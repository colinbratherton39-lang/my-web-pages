📜 How to Play

  1. Click Spin All Four or spin each wheel separately.
  2. Watch the wheels spin and stop at different times. Each one selects a Subject, Style, Medium, or Background.
  3. Once all four wheels have stopped, your completed image prompt will appear automatically under Your Image Prompt.
  4. Click Copy Prompt, then paste the final prompt into your chosen AI image generator.
  5. Generate your image and see how the four selections come together! Spin again for a new creative challenge.

Challenge rule: Your generated prompt includes all four roulette selections. No need to write your own prompt.
